# BrightPlan

> BrightPlan is an award-winning global financial wellness platform that helps enterprises give employees personalized financial guidance — improving financial wellbeing, engagement, and the impact of their benefits.

BrightPlan delivers Total Financial Wellness for the enterprise: a self-service digital platform, an AI-powered coach, and access to unbiased financial planners, backed by global support. It is built for HR and benefits leaders who want to maximize the impact of their financial wellness benefit and help employees make confident financial decisions.
